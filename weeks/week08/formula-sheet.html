The formulas

This is the last page of the midterm, word for word, and the sheet attached to the Brightspace quiz. It has every formula the exam needs. Bring a pen and nothing else: no notes and no calculator.

  • Breadth-first: complete, optimal for unit cost, time and space O(bd).
  • Depth-first: complete only in finite spaces, not optimal, space O(bm).
  • Uniform cost: optimal, expands in order of g(n), time and space O(b1+⌊C*/ε⌋).
  • A*: f(n) = g(n) + h(n); admissible h ≤ h*; consistent h(n) ≤ c(n, n′) + h(n′).
  • Alpha–beta: best case O(bd/2), worst case O(bd).
  • Bellman expectation: Vπ(s) = Σs′ T(s, π(s), s′)[R(s, π(s), s′) + γVπ(s′)].
  • Bellman optimality: V*(s) = maxa Σs′ T(s, a, s′)[R(s, a, s′) + γV*(s′)].
  • Value iteration: Vk+1(s) ← maxa Σs′ T(s, a, s′)[R(s, a, s′) + γVk(s′)].
  • Q-learning: Q(s, a) ← Q(s, a) + α[r + γ maxa′ Q(s′, a′) − Q(s, a)].
  • TD(0): V(s) ← V(s) + α[r + γV(s′) − V(s)].
  • Effective horizon: approximately 1/(1 − γ).
