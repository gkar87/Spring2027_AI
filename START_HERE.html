Eight things to know

  1. Unzip the folder first

    Extract the whole zip before you open anything (Windows: right-click it and choose Extract All; macOS: double-click it). Keep the folders as they are: the pages link to each other and to the assets folder.

  2. Open the course home

    Open index.html in the extracted folder. It has the schedule and a link to every module's slides, playground, lab, reflection and paper critique, and to both exams. syllabus.html has the grading and the course policies.

  3. In week 1: set up Python

    The labs need Python 3.11 or newer with NumPy, on your own computer or in a free Google Colab or GitHub Codespaces account. No paid software is needed. The labs page (labs/index.html) has links to catch up on Python, and the data for every lab is in labs/data, explained in labs/data/README.md. Before Lab 5, make its image data once: in labs/data/lab5, run python3 make_dataset.py (a few seconds; it builds the same file for everyone).

  4. Every week

    Read the module's slides (weeks/weekNN/index.html; press P in a deck to save it as a PDF) and the reading on the schedule. Section 01 meets on Fridays, 8:30 to 11:10 a.m. CT, at the Missouri Innovation Campus (MIC); the room will be announced. Section 02 follows the recorded lecture posted for the week. There is no discussion board and no participation grade.

  5. Submitting work

    Submit labs, reflections and critiques in Brightspace. Each lab sheet says what to hand in and how to check your own output, and reflections/index.html says what to read and write for each reflection. Read the policy on AI tools in syllabus.html before lab 1: use is allowed for some tasks and must always be disclosed. Grades: reflections 10%, labs 30%, paper critiques 10%, midterm 20%, final exam 30%.

  6. Deadlines

    Sunday, 11:59 p.m. Central, unless the assignment says otherwise. Labs, reflections and critiques lose 10% per day for up to three days, then are not accepted. There are no free late days. The midterm and the final exam have fixed dates: if one is impossible for you, contact Dr. Kar before it.

  7. Midterm

    Friday, March 5, 8:30 to 10:30 a.m. CT, on modules 1 to 7. Section 01 sits it at MIC; Section 02 sits the same paper online, proctored. Allowed: one handwritten 8.5 × 11 in note card, both sides, and the formula sheet handed out with the exam. No calculators. A practice midterm with answers is in weeks/week08/practice.html.

  8. Final exam

    Friday, May 7, 8:30 to 10:30 a.m. CT, in finals week, on the whole course weighted to modules 9 to 15. Section 01 sits it at MIC; Section 02 takes it in Brightspace at the same time, proctored. The final exam page (weeks/week15/final-exam.html) has the parts, what to bring and the formula sheet.

Using the slides

In the slides, use the arrow keys or the Previous and Next buttons to move. Press O, or the Slides button, to see every slide at once and jump to any of them. R replays a slide's animation and P prints or saves as PDF. On a phone, turn it sideways for larger slides. Every page opens with a plain double-click; nothing needs a server.
