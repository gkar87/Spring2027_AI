Required appendix to the capstone report. Two pages at most, written for the person deciding whether to trust the system — not for your grader.

Intended use

Two or three sentences on who should use this, for what, and with what oversight. Be narrow. "A research prototype for comparing retrievers on the course corpus" is a usable statement; "general question answering" is not.

Limitations and failure modes

Three to five bullets, specific. Each names a condition under which the system is unreliable and, where you know it, why. The failure case from the report belongs here as one bullet, in condensed form.

Ethical considerations

Who could be harmed if this system were used outside its intended use, and what would that harm look like? One paragraph, honest rather than ceremonial. If the answer is genuinely "very little, because the scope is narrow", say that and say why.
