Weeks

Each week has slides and either a lab or a reading reflection. Weeks 3, 6, 10 and 13 also have a playground: an explorer and exercises that run in your browser and check your answers. Two paper critiques are due in weeks 9 and 14. The midterm is in week 8 and the final exam in finals week.

How the course is graded, and its policies, are in the syllabus: reflections 10%, labs 30%, paper critiques 10%, midterm 20%, final exam 30%.

You submit everything in Brightspace. Each lab sheet lists exactly what goes in a lab submission, and the reflections page says what a reflection must contain.
